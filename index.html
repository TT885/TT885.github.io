RESEARCH PROFILE

About me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Xiaofeng Gao (高晓沨).

My research focuses on mobile computing, spatial decision-making, and online optimization. I study how to assign tasks, plan routes, and schedule limited resources in mobile and crowdsourced systems, with an emphasis on fairness, efficiency, and practical constraints.

我关注移动计算中的任务分配、路径规划与在线决策，希望把算法与真实系统中的需求连接起来。

Spatial crowdsourcingOnline algorithmsRouting & schedulingMobile systems

I welcome research conversations and collaborations, and am interested in overseas postdoctoral opportunities starting in 2027. Get in touch.
